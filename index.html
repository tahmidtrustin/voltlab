LEARNING PATH

Master the Basics

Start from the fundamentals and gradually build your understanding of electrical circuits.

01 — FUNDAMENTALS

Electric Charge

Learn what electric charge is and how positive and negative charges behave.

02 — CORE CONCEPT

Voltage

Understand potential difference and why voltage drives current through a circuit.

03 — CORE CONCEPT

Electric Current

Explore the movement of electric charge and understand current direction.

04 — CORE CONCEPT

Resistance

Learn how resistance affects current and how resistors behave in circuits.

05 — CIRCUITS

Series & Parallel

Compare series and parallel circuits and understand their electrical behavior.

06 — LAWS

Kirchhoff's Laws

Explore KCL and KVL and understand how they are applied to electrical networks.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Why We Built VoltLab

A student-built digital learning project designed to make Basic Electricity more understandable and engaging.

🎓 Academic Project

VoltLab is developed as a Basic Electricity course project by students of the Department of Computer Science & Engineering at Jamalpur Science and Technology University.

⚡ Our Goal

Our goal is to present electrical concepts through an interactive digital environment so that students can learn concepts, visualize circuits and practice problems more effectively.
