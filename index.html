About VoltLab

A modern approach to learning basic electricity.

VoltLab is an interactive web-based electrical learning platform designed to make fundamental electricity concepts more visual, practical and engaging.


Instead of only reading formulas, students can calculate electrical values, visualize circuits, experiment with LED brightness, analyze Kirchhoff's laws, build circuits and investigate electrical faults.


The platform also introduces a local progress system that turns learning activities into an interactive journey.

Conclusion

Turning electrical theory into an interactive experience.

VoltLab demonstrates how modern web technology can transform traditional electrical concepts into a more visual, interactive and engaging learning experience.


Through interactive calculations, virtual experiments, circuit challenges and future simulation tools, the platform is designed to help students understand not only the formulas, but also what actually happens inside an electrical circuit.


This project represents the collaborative work, creativity and technical learning of CSE students at Jamalpur Science and Technology University.
