How to connect

1. Add components to the board.

2. Click one component terminal, then click another terminal to connect a wire.

3. Connect a complete circuit with a battery and load.

Connection tip

Click two terminals to create a wire. Click a connected wire to select it, then use Delete last wire to remove the most recent connection. Use Cancel wire to clear an unfinished connection.
